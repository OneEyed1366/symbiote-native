# @symbiote-native/sharing

## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- 80ed828: Add `@symbiote-native/sharing` — a framework-agnostic wrapper around `expo-sharing` (built on
  `expo-modules-core`, never the `expo` meta-package), reachable identically from React, Vue, and
  Angular. Opens the platform share sheet for a local file: `shareAsync(url, options?)` with
  `mimeType` (Android), `UTI` (iOS), `dialogTitle` and the iPad popover `anchor`, plus
  `isAvailableAsync()`.

  **Outgoing share only — the incoming half of `expo-sharing` is deliberately not ported.**
  `useIncomingShare`, `getSharedPayloads`, `getResolvedSharedPayloadsAsync` and
  `clearSharedPayloads` receive files that other apps share _into_ the app, and reaching them needs
  a whole iOS Share Extension target: upstream's config plugin generates a second Xcode target with
  its own `Info.plist`, entitlements and a shared App Group ID, plus Android intent filters. That is
  native app-extension scaffolding — the same category `expo-widgets` sits in — and this repo has no
  native-target scaffolding story yet, so the surface is documented as out of scope rather than
  shipped half-working.

  All three adapter entry points are plain re-exports of the core: both exports are stateless free
  functions with no per-instance state and no event stream, so there is nothing for a hook,
  composable, or service to wrap — the same shape `@symbiote-native/secure-store` has. The incoming
  half is precisely the part that would have needed one.

  Android registration is generated by `@symbiote-native/expo-modules-link` from the package's
  `native-link.json`. No iOS usage-description string is needed — the share sheet asks for no
  permission — and the `SharingFileProvider` plus the API-30 `<queries>` block ship in
  `expo-sharing`'s own manifest and merge in automatically.

  One deviation from upstream: `shareAsync` rejects an empty or non-string `url` before the native
  call, so a bad argument fails with a readable message instead of as an argument-conversion failure
  on iOS or a generic "Failed to share the file" on Android.

### Patch Changes

- 80ed828: Stop publishing co-located test files. These packages ship `src/` because the Angular entry's
  `default` export condition resolves back into it, which also swept in every `*.test.ts` beside
  those sources — 24% of tracking-transparency's unpacked size, 11% of web-browser's. `files` now
  excludes the `.test.`/`.spec.`/`.detox.` suffixes, and an eslint rule keeps them out.
