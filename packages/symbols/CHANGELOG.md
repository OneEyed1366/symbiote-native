## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- [#93](https://github.com/OneEyed1366/symbiote-native/pull/93) [`5435556`](https://github.com/OneEyed1366/symbiote-native/commit/5435556869929fe5fd4798ac8f3dedb2fe893851) Thanks [@OneEyed1366](https://github.com/OneEyed1366)! - Add `@symbiote-native/symbols`: `SymbolView` (SF Symbols on iOS, Material Symbols on Android through `@symbiote-native/font`), the `androidWeights/*` fonts and `unstable_getMaterialSymbolSourceAsync` on React, Vue, Svelte, Solid and Angular, and a `--symbols` layer in the CLI.
